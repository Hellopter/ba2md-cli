# 3.x.2.6 Integration Design · Conditional

Read this constraint when the requirement involves messaging, scheduled jobs, third-party services, cross-system synchronization, or asynchronous processing.

## Research and Writing

- Identify integration point, direction, owner, trigger, and payload.
- Cover delivery semantics, ordering, idempotency, timeout, retry, dead-letter handling, compensation, and manual recovery.
- Identify configuration/secret ownership and observability.
- Draw responsibility boundaries across sources.

## Quality Gate

- Failure-handling ownership is explicit.
- Existing event and job names are VERIFIED.
- A new integration explains why existing seams are insufficient.
